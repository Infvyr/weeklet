<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Verify REL-03 — Currency symbol propagates to all screens</name>
  <what-built>
    Plan 07-01 threaded `currencySymbol` from SettingsBloc through all list and
    stats widgets. Every amount display that previously showed hardcoded 'MDL'
    should now show the user-selected currency symbol.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Run the app on a device or simulator:

    ```bash
    cd "/Users/vasilenovatchii/My Projects/Flutter/Weeklet"
    fvm flutter run
    ```

    Perform these steps in order:

    **Step 1 — Change currency to EUR:**
    1. Open the app and navigate to Settings (bottom nav or settings icon).
    2. Tap 'Currency' (or equivalent setting).
    3. Select EUR. Confirm the settings subtitle or selection shows 'EUR'.

    **Step 2 — Verify Expenses screen:**
    4. Navigate to the Expenses screen.
    5. Confirm that expense amounts in list rows show 'EUR', not 'MDL'.
       FAIL if any amount shows 'MDL' or any other hardcoded symbol.

    **Step 3 — Verify Income screen:**
    6. Navigate to the Income screen.
    7. Confirm the IncomeTotalCard (top card showing total) displays 'EUR'.
    8. Confirm income list rows show 'EUR'.
       FAIL if either shows 'MDL'.

    **Step 4 — Verify Statistics screen, Monthly tab:**
    9. Navigate to Statistics. Ensure the Monthly tab is active.
    10. Confirm StatsBalanceCard (large card with balance) shows 'EUR'.
    11. Confirm the two StatsSmallCards (income and expenses summary cards) show 'EUR'.
    12. Confirm the ModernDonutChart center total shows 'EUR'.
    13. Confirm CategoryDetailsList rows show 'EUR'.
        FAIL if any of these shows 'MDL'.

    **Step 5 — Verify Statistics screen, Annual tab:**
    14. Switch to the Annual tab on the Statistics screen.
    15. Tap a bar in the AnnualGroupedBarChart to reveal the tooltip.
    16. Confirm the tooltip shows 'EUR', not 'MDL'.
    17. Scroll down to the MonthlyExpensesList (Monthly Breakdown card).
    18. Confirm the bar amount labels show 'EUR'.
        FAIL if either shows 'MDL'.

    **Step 6 — Revert and re-verify:**
    19. Navigate back to Settings. Change currency back to MDL.
    20. Navigate to Expenses screen and confirm amounts show 'MDL'.
    21. Navigate to Statistics, Annual tab, confirm tooltip shows 'MDL'.
        FAIL if currency did not update reactively.

    **Pass criteria:**
    - All amount displays in all 3 screens show the selected currency symbol
    - Symbol updates reactively when changed in Settings (no app restart needed)

    **Fail criteria:**
    - Any screen shows 'MDL' after EUR was selected
    - Tooltip in AnnualGroupedBarChart still shows 'MDL' after EUR was selected
    - Reverting to MDL does not update the displayed symbol
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>
    Type "REL-03 PASS" if all 6 steps above pass.
    Type "REL-03 FAIL: [describe which screen/widget still shows wrong currency]"

  <name>Task 2: Verify STAT-03 — Annual bar chart updates after CRUD</name>
  <what-built>
    Plan 07-02 removed the `shouldRefetchEvolution` guard from StatsBloc so that
    getEvolutionStatsUseCase is called unconditionally on every LoadMonthlyStats
    event, including same-year CRUD dispatches from ExpenseBloc and IncomeBloc.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Use a running app (from Task 1, or relaunch if needed):

    **Step 1 — Baseline the annual chart:**
    1. Navigate to Statistics. Switch to the Annual tab.
    2. Note the current state of the bar chart for the current month (e.g.,
       October bar shows a specific height or is absent).

    **Step 2 — Add an expense and verify chart updates:**
    3. Navigate to the Expenses screen.
    4. Add a new expense in any category, any amount (e.g., 500 in current
       month). Confirm it saves and appears in the list.
    5. Navigate back to Statistics. Switch to the Annual tab if it is not active.
    6. Confirm the bar for the current month has increased (or a bar now appears
       where none was). The chart must reflect the new expense WITHOUT requiring
       navigation to a different year and back.
       FAIL if the bar is unchanged (stale cache).

    **Step 3 — Add income and verify:**
    7. Navigate to the Income screen.
    8. Add a new income entry (any amount) in the current month.
    9. Navigate back to Statistics, Annual tab.
    10. Confirm the income bar for the current month increased.
        FAIL if unchanged.

    **Step 4 — Delete and verify:**
    11. Delete the test expense added in Step 4.
    12. Navigate back to Statistics, Annual tab.
    13. Confirm the expense bar for the current month decreased.
        FAIL if the bar retained the deleted expense.

    **Pass criteria:**
    - Annual bar chart reflects each CRUD operation immediately (within one
      navigation back to the Statistics screen)
    - No year navigation required to see updates

    **Fail criteria:**
    - Annual bar chart shows stale data after expense/income CRUD
    - Chart only updates after navigating to a different year and back
  </how-to-verify>
