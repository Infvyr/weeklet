# weeklet

Weeklet is a Flutter app for simple personal finance management.   You can add your weekly expenses for each month, divided into categories such as   daily expenses, utilities, internet/TV, other, shopping, savings, and car.   The app automatically calculates weekly and monthly totals, and in the statistics section   you can see an annual overview—totals by category and total income.   Ideal for those who want control, clarity, and financial simplicity.  Translated with DeepL.com (free version)
